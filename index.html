STANDARD ISSUE

Your drone carries the big gun.

Field weapons do not go in your hands. They go to your DSCO, a Deployable Support Combat Operator: a drone that follows you, holds the weapon, and fires when you fire.

Pick up a railgun and the drone brings it along until the ammunition runs out, then drops it and waits for the next one. You are never unarmed and never stuck with a bad pickup.

FIELD MANUAL

What is in the box.

60+

Gear that compounds

Stacking items, plus consumables for when the plan stops working. Effects multiply rather than replace. A run that begins with a slightly faster reload can end with a screen that is no longer strictly legible.

5

Bosses that fill the screen

The Foremen are four industrial emplacements working a shared metronome, and the rhythm tightens every time one of them goes down. The others are worse.

2–4

Player co-op

Invites go through Steam. No lobby codes, no IP addresses, no port forwarding. A death is not the end of the run while a teammate is still standing: you redeploy at the next zone boundary.

4

Operation tiers

Each one unlocked by finishing the one before it. At the top tier, anything that survives long enough gets angry. That includes the bosses.
