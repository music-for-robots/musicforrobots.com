Every run assembles a fresh chain of rooms, hands you whatever the drop tables felt like handing you, and asks you to make it work.

The aliens are numerous, expressive, and extremely committed. So are you.

STANDARD ISSUE

Your drone carries the big gun.

Field weapons do not go in your hands. They go to your DSCO, a Deployable Support Combat Operator: a drone that follows you, holds the weapon, and fires when you fire.

It keeps the gun until the ammunition runs out, then drops it and waits for the next one. Your own weapon never runs dry, so you are never unarmed and never stuck with a bad pickup.

FIELD MANUAL

What is in the box.

60+

Gear that compounds

Stacking items, plus consumables for when the plan stops working. Effects multiply rather than replace. A run that begins with a slightly faster reload can end with a screen that is no longer strictly legible.

5

Bosses that fill the screen

The Foremen are four industrial emplacements working a shared metronome, and the rhythm tightens every time one of them goes down. The others are worse.

2–4

Player co-op

Invites go through Steam. No lobby codes, no IP addresses, no port forwarding. A death is not the end of the run while a teammate is still standing: you redeploy at the next zone boundary.

4

Operation tiers

Each one unlocked by finishing the one before it. At the top tier, anything that survives long enough gets angry. That includes the bosses.

EASYNORMALHARDINVADERS MUST DIE

Also: full controller support with rebinding, an uncapped framerate, and a WARDEN terminal that answers when you type at it.

REQUISITIONS

A selection from the catalogue.

Everything stacks, and the stacks multiply. Twelve of the sixty-odd items in the field.

UNCOMMON

Split Barrel

Fire one extra shot per stack, spread in a fan. Each extra shot deals 50% damage.

// Barrel splitter, non-regulation. Quartermaster declines to certify grouping but notes 'more is more.'

XENOTECH

Army of Me

A mirror of you fires the same shots 0.5 s later. +1 mirror per stack; each inherits every item you carry.

// Phase-shifted echo of the operative. Tactical duplicate; no manifest assignment required.

RARE

Mr. Self Destruct

On dropping below 50% HP, detonate a blast around you for 80 damage (+80 per stack). Recharges between rooms.

// Subdermal kinetic-release implant. Triggers on threshold pulse rate; not user-controllable.

XENOTECH

Fifty-Fifty Crown

Every item with a proc chance becomes a coin flip.

// WARDEN probability-bias regulator. Collapses weighted outcomes to an even two-state; a paired unit pins the result. Additional units are decorative.

UNCOMMON

Echo Beach

20% (+20% per stack) chance on hit to fire a 50%-damage copy of the shot at another enemy.

// Fragmenting secondary charge. One round fired, two rounds accounted for.

XENOTECH

Bombtrack

Kills leave a 1.5 s fuse. Detonation: 60 damage per stack.

// Residual ordnance signature in target tissue. Detonation interval consistent across forensic samples.

RARE

Stomach Parasite

6% (+6% per stack) chance to absorb an incoming bullet and heal 5 HP.

// Xenobiotic symbiote, classification pending. Host retention rate: 94%.

Seek and Destroy

// Self-calibrating micro-fin retrofit. Point of aim is now advisory.

Wandering Star
